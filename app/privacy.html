Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

1. Who we are

This policy applies to the Vivekanand Law Firm mobile application and web app (“the App”). The data fiduciary is Vivekanand Law Firm, led by Adv. Vivek Shrivastava. Contact: adv.vivekshrivastava@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

The App has no user accounts and does not run any server of its own. The App does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, location or contacts.

When you choose to send a consultation request or a store order, the details you type (name, mobile number, city, address, description of your matter, items ordered) are placed into a message which opens in WhatsApp, your e-mail app or your UPI app. That information reaches us only when you send that message yourself.

Your cart, saved delivery address and order history are stored only on your device and can be erased by clearing the App's data.

3. How we use your information

Information you send us is used only to respond to your enquiry, schedule a consultation, or process and deliver your order. We do not sell or share it with third parties, save courier partners for delivery, or where required by law.

4. Payments

UPI payments are made in your own UPI app (e.g. Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM). The App does not see or store your bank, card or UPI PIN details.

5. Confidentiality

Communications made for the purpose of seeking legal advice are treated as confidential and privileged under S. 132 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023. Please do not send sensitive documents through the consultation form; share them after the consultation is confirmed.

6. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may request access to, correction of, or erasure of personal data you have sent us, and withdraw consent, by writing to the e-mail address above.

7. Third-party services

Links to WhatsApp, e-mail, UPI apps, eCourts and court websites open those services, which are governed by their own privacy policies.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children. Store purchases should be made by adults.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will reflect the latest version.
